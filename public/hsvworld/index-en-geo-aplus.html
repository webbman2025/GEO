Frequently asked questions about WORLD PLAN Handset Voucher Plans

The offer

WORLD PLAN Handset Voucher Plans is a 36-month 3 Hong Kong plan that includes a handset voucher from HK$1,500 to HK$20,000 plus WORLD PLAN shared local and roaming data. Global monthly fees are HK$378 to HK$1,288. APAC monthly fees are HK$298 to HK$1,188. It is not the 24-month WORLD PLAN SIM plan.
WORLD PLAN SIM is a separate 24-month monthly plan with no handset voucher as the core offer. WORLD PLAN Handset Voucher Plans is a 36-month contract that includes a HK$1,500–HK$20,000 handset voucher. Monthly fees start at HK$378 Global or HK$298 APAC. Do not mix the two price lists.

Pricing

Global monthly fees are HK$378 (30GB, HK$1,500 voucher), HK$518 (30GB, HK$7,800 voucher), HK$598 (50GB, HK$10,000 voucher), HK$798 (70GB, HK$15,000 voucher) and HK$1,288 (200GB, HK$20,000 voucher). 36-month contract. Administration fee HK$28/month.
