WORLD PLAN Handset Voucher Plan - Travel is part of everyday life. Designed for Hongkongers riding the northbound trend and taking frequent overseas trips, this 5G plan lets you use your monthly data locally and for roaming across 222 destinations worldwide. It also includes a handset voucher, plus a free flight ticket or hotel voucher, for a getaway anytime.

Global Shared Data vs Asia Pacific Shared Data – Choose the One That Fits You

Global Shared Data: Hong Kong and global shared data, with monthly fees starting from HK$378, covering 222 destinations around the globe.
Asia Pacific Shared Data: Hong Kong and Asia Pacific shared data, with monthly fees starting from HK$298, covering popular destinations throughout Asia-Pacific region.
