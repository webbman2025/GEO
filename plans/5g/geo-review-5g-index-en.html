Critical gaps for AI citation

Gap 1 — No BLUF box

Current (repeated 3×): “Port-in to 3HK 5G designated SIM plan, enjoy FREE 6-month for 5G plan at just $100/30GB and extra free 10 million AI tokens!”
Proposed BLUF (paste under breadcrumb, before plan tiles)

3HK 5G Monthly SIM Plans: Subscribe online with local data from 30GB to 100GB (then infinite data up to 1Mbps). Basic monthly fee HK$124–HK$228 on 12, 24 or 30-month contracts (HK$28/month administration fee applies). Port-in customers on a 30-month contract enjoy FREE 6-month monthly fee, admin fee discount, and 10 million AI Tokens — average monthly fee from HK$100 for the 30GB plan. Includes 3,000 local voice minutes and Chinese Mainland–Macau shared data. eSIM and physical SIM available. Terms apply.

Gap 2 — FAQ buried in accordion

Surface 6–8 page-specific FAQs visibly (match schema draft). Suggested questions:

  • How much is the 3HK 5G plan with 30GB local data?
  • What is the port-in offer and average monthly fee from HK$100?
  • What happens after I use 30GB / 50GB / 60GB / 100GB?
  • How do I subscribe online and how long until I receive my SIM?
  • Can I port in before my current contract ends?
  • What is eSIM and why choose it over a physical SIM?
  • Are there hidden charges?
  • How do MoneyBack members get extra local data?
